Safety First

How we support a safer platform

Our safety page communicates expectations, responsible platform use, and trust-building practices.

Secure Accounts

Users are encouraged to maintain secure login credentials and keep account information up to date.

Clear Communication

We support respectful and professional communication between users and businesses.

Responsible Use

The platform should be used honestly and for legitimate estimate-related requests and services.

For Customers

Customer safety guidelines

  • Provide accurate project information
  • Use professional communication
  • Review service details carefully
For Businesses

Business safety guidelines

  • Respond honestly and professionally
  • Keep business details accurate
  • Respect customer privacy

Report a concern

If a user experiences inappropriate or suspicious activity, the concern can be reported to the support team for review.
