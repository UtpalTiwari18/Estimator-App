About Estimator

Everything important about Estimator, in one place.

Estimator helps drivers connect with local automotive service providers in a faster, easier, and more organized way. Below are the main sections you wanted inside the body.

01

About Us

Estimator is a platform built to make car care simpler. Instead of calling multiple businesses one by one, customers can submit what service they need and connect with nearby providers more efficiently. Our goal is to make the process cleaner, faster, and easier for both customers and businesses.

02

How It Works

Customers describe the service they need, add their location details, and submit a request. Businesses in the same area can then respond with quotes. This saves time, reduces confusion, and gives customers a more convenient way to compare options.

03

Become a Business

Estimator also helps automotive businesses grow. Workshops, mechanics, detailers, and mobile service providers can join the platform, showcase their services, and receive leads from customers who are already looking for help nearby.
